# Seedance-Prompt für «Untitled»

Modus Multi-Referenz, 9:16, 30 s, 720p. `@Image 1`: Paul Cézanne, «Montagne Sainte-Victoire» (späte Fassung um 1904–06, gemeinfrei).
Rothko und Kapoor nur über ihre Bildsprache beschrieben, nicht genannt; die Fahrerin bleibt anonym (Gesicht verdeckt), der Bezug zu Florentina Holzinger steht nur im Intro.
Geschrieben mit dem Skill `seedance-25-prompting`.

```
[Generation Goal]
A 30-second continuous sequence filmed as real physical space and real materials: a
painting by Paul Cézanne breaks apart into planes of color, the planes become a turning
carousel of color-field paintings, the carousel drains into a black whirlpool in a
gallery floor, and a performer rides a jet ski on the whirlpool.

[Materials]
@Image 1 defines the painting Mont Sainte-Victoire: use its exact composition, colors
and brushstrokes as the starting frame. Do not use its frame or the museum wall.

[Stage 1]
0-7 seconds: @Image 1 fills the frame as a real oil painting on canvas, raised impasto
catching low light. The camera pushes in slowly. The small patches of paint lift off the
canvas one after another and float apart as thin flat planes of ochre, green and blue in
a dim space.
End state: dozens of floating color planes drift in darkness, the canvas behind them
empty.

[Stage 2]
7-15 seconds: The planes merge into eight tall canvases, each holding soft-edged
rectangles of maroon, deep red, orange and black with gently glowing borders. The
canvases form a ring around the camera and turn slowly like a carousel in a dim gallery
with a polished concrete floor. The camera stays at the center of the ring.
End state: the carousel turns faster and the colors smear into one dark red ring.

[Stage 3]
15-22 seconds: The ring of color sinks into the floor and becomes a churning whirlpool of
black water set into a bright white gallery floor, the surface spiraling down into a deep
center. The camera cranes up to a high overhead view.
End state: overhead view of a perfectly round black vortex in the white floor.

[Stage 4]
22-30 seconds: A woman performer in a black wetsuit, her face hidden behind spray and
dark goggles, rides a red jet ski fast along the rim of the vortex, carving a tight
circle, white spray arcing in curtains over the black water. The camera slowly orbits
from above, following her.
End state: the jet ski keeps circling the rim, spray glittering in the light.

[Style]
Shot on ARRI Alexa, 35mm lens, subtle film grain. Real texture throughout: canvas weave,
oil impasto, droplets and foam on the water, wet sheen on the jet ski. One light source
per space: low warm side light on the painting, soft overhead skylight in the gallery,
cool even daylight on the white floor.

[Maintain Consistency]
One continuous camera path at a calm speed, the jet ski and performer stable in shape.
No drift, no deformation, no flickering.

Audio: <a soft papery rustle as the paint lifts off>, then <a low hum of the turning
carousel>, then <rushing, swirling water>, then <the roar of a jet ski engine and
splashing spray>.
No background music, no voice-over, no text overlay, no captions, no logos.
```
