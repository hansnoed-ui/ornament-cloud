Zu seiner Zeit

Plateaus on Operation, Art, Society and Artificial Communication

7 cycles × 7 stanzas = 49 stanzas

Begin with chance →

I Operation

From occurrence to time: operations become a history only in their connection.

  1. 1 The Bridge

    It is not the world that grounds the connection – the connection invents the world that afterwards makes it appear necessary.

  2. 2 The Event

    What has happened has happened; its history changes with every connection.

  3. 3 The Connection

    Continuity is not a state – it is the most improbable of all operations.

  4. 4 The Observer

    The observer can observe the distinction of their present observing only later.

  5. 5 The Blind Spot

    The blind spot is not the end of knowledge – it is its beginning.

  6. 6 The Delay

    The present is the art of overlooking its own delay.

  7. 7 Time

    Time is the condition that something other than its repetition can follow an operation.

II Recursion

How the past remains effective in traces, practices and narratives.

  1. 8 The Store

    Storage keeps something available; resumption gives it a new present.

  2. 9 Memory

    Memory makes the past effective again in changed practices.

  3. 10 Remembering

    Remembering gives what has happened a present, verifiable shape.

  4. 11 The Trace

    The trace holds a past effect materially without fixing its meaning.

  5. 12 Forgetting

    Forgetting changes what remains within reach, and for whom.

  6. 13 The Archive

    The archive keeps and orders what can be found again in the future.

  7. 14 History

    History interprets the past anew and remains bound to its traces.

III Address

How recognisability is socially attributed and becomes expectable.

  1. 15 The Address

    The address makes social attribution possible across absence.

  2. 16 The Name

    The name makes absence addressable.

  3. 17 The Person

    The person is the memory of an address.

  4. 18 The Role

    The role makes expectations independent of persons.

  5. 19 Expectation

    Expectations order what can come as a surprise at an address.

  6. 20 Responsibility

    Responsibility binds consequences to an address.

  7. 21 Recognition

    Recognition gives an address social validity.

IV Coupling

How separate orders become effective for one another without uniting.

  1. 22 The Boundary

    Only what remains separate can touch.

  2. 23 Touch

    Touch is the boundary in the form of an event.

  3. 24 Irritation

    Irritation is an outside effect in a form of one's own.

  4. 25 The Body

    The body turns outside effects into sensitivity.

  5. 26 Perception

    Perception makes the boundary between body and consciousness questionable.

  6. 27 Translation

    Translation is repetition without identity.

  7. 28 Coupling

    Coupling stabilises effects at boundaries whose emergence itself remains to be questioned.

V Art

How differences of perception remain socially reusable.

  1. 29 The Material

    Material is the resistance through which form becomes possible.

  2. 30 The Form

    Form makes difference reusable.

  3. 31 The Work

    A work remains recognisable as a particular form; an artistic address can carry several works.

  4. 32 Style

    Style is the recurrence of a difference as expectation.

  5. 33 The Audience

    The audience cultivates the sensitivity of separate perceptions.

  6. 34 The Exhibition

    The exhibition orders relations before it orders meanings.

  7. 35 Art

    Art keeps perception socially reusable by keeping its difference open.

VI Platform

How recognisability is turned into probability – and probability is returned again as environment.

  1. 36 The Profile

    The profile turns addressable traces into calculable recognisability.

  2. 37 Information

    Information arises when a difference has consequences within a particular order.

  3. 38 Probability

    Probability turns open contingency into an unequally weighted future.

  4. 39 The Algorithm

    Algorithmic ranking distributes unequal chances of the next connection.

  5. 40 The Recommendation

    The recommendation returns calculated probability as a possible next world.

  6. 41 The Feed

    The feed makes prediction the present environment of its next verification.

  7. 42 The Platform

    The platform returns calculated possibilities as environment and processes their consequences.

VII Future

Under what conditions, despite these reductions, going on otherwise remains possible.

  1. 43 Identity

    Identity makes change attributable across time.

  2. 44 Possibility

    Possibility is the present reachability of a connection not yet actualised.

  3. 45 Contingency

    Contingency becomes a chance for the future where alternatives remain within reach and structures remain revisable.

  4. 46 The Interruption

    An interruption opens up the future only if something can connect differently after it.

  5. 47 The New

    New is what changes what is possible afterwards.

  6. 48 Freedom

    Freedom is the ability to take up, reject or reshape structured contingency.

  7. 49 The Future

    The future is the preserved room to go on otherwise than is already expected.
