The Bridge

It is not the world that grounds the connection – the connection invents the world that afterwards makes it appear necessary.

Every theory begins with a bridge. It links concepts before it even knows whether anything is waiting for it on the other side. Therein lies its real daring. Its first step is not truth but the presumption of a connection. Only when later operations take up this connection again does it acquire the appearance of a necessary order. What we call an origin is therefore usually nothing other than the first successful connection. Bridges are not built because banks exist; banks come into being because bridges have learned to remember their own direction.
