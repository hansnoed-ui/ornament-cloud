The Material

Material is the resistance through which form becomes possible.

Material is not the mute stuff to which an artistic idea gives form after the fact. Wood splinters, paint runs, fabric tightens, metal fatigues, digital systems produce errors and dependencies. In the work, material answers by enabling, deflecting or rejecting intentions. Its resistances are not external obstacles but conditions for something to emerge that was not fully designed beforehand. Even after it has been worked, material remains active: it ages, changes its surface and reacts to light, moisture, touch and time. Art begins where this movement of its own is not eliminated but admitted as participation.
