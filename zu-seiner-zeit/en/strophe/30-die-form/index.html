The Form

Form makes difference reusable.

Form is not a casing forced onto a material after the fact. It is a provisional order in which material, space, time, attention and expectation are related to one another so that a difference can recur. A form does not remain the same because it is unchanged. It remains recognisable although its execution, its context and its meaning change. It is precisely this portability that makes it socially effective: forms can be quoted, shifted, forgotten and taken up again. Where material produces resistance, form makes this resistance distinguishable and available for further operations. It stores nothing; it organises reusability.
