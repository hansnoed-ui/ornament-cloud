Possibility

Possibility is the present reachability of a connection not yet actualised.

Possibility is not a stock of invisible worlds behind reality. It is carried along in the present when a connection appears realisable, conceivable or expectable without already being actualised. Every structure limits such possibilities, otherwise no selection would be possible. But it does not necessarily destroy them. A door opens up other connections than a wall; a language other sentences than silence; an institution other decisions than a private whim. Possibility is therefore structured openness. It consists not in the arbitrary but in a present order admitting more than one continuation of its own. The future begins where this more remains practically within reach.
