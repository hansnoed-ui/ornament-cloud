The Profile

The profile turns addressable traces into calculable recognisability.

An address makes reachable; a profile makes recognisable. It collects past clicks, purchases, routes, contacts or dwell times and assigns them to an address. What emerges is not an image of the whole person but a selection of comparable features. What appears as a pattern can be compared with other patterns and used for future decisions. Depending on the purpose, the same person is broken down into different calculable segments. Memory thereby shifts towards prediction: the past is used again to make next connections more probable. The addressed human being and the calculated profile do not coincide; yet the profile can determine what that person encounters next.
