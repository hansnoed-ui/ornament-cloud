Information

Information arises when a difference has consequences within a particular order.

A data set can be stored; a selection from it becomes informative only for a particular question and a possible connection. The same click can have a different effect in a music recommendation than in a fraud check. Between recording and information lie rules of comparing, weighting and using. These rules are not neutral themselves: they help decide which difference stands out and which goes unconsidered. Platforms automate parts of this selection and use its results for later calculations. Data are certainly a material and technical starting point in this. Information here designates the effect of a difference within an order of observation and action.
