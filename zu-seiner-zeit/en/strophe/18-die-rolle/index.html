The Role

The role makes expectations independent of persons.

A role is not a mask behind which the real person hides. It is an order of expectations that already exists before anyone takes it on. Teacher, mother, patient, artist or judge do not designate properties but positions from which certain actions become probable and others call for explanation. Roles relieve persons because they need not bring their whole individuality into every situation. At the same time they set limits: whoever takes on a role is measured against expectations that did not come into being with them. In this way the role makes social relationships repeatable by making persons interchangeable without replacing them entirely.
