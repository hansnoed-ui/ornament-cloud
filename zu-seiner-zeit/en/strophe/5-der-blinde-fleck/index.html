The Blind Spot

The blind spot is not the end of knowledge – it is its beginning.

Every operation takes place under conditions it cannot itself observe. Not because it lacks information, but because its own form determines what may appear as information at all. The blind spot is therefore not a deficit of knowledge but its precondition. Whoever wanted to remove it would have to step outside their own operation – and in doing so would produce a new distinction with a new blind spot. Knowledge widens its field of vision not by overcoming blindness, but by learning to vary its own limits productively.
