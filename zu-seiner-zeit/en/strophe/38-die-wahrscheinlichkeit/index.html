Probability

Probability turns open contingency into an unequally weighted future.

Probability does not remove contingency. It orders it. Between what has happened and what could happen, a distribution of unequal expectations arises. The improbable remains possible but is treated differently from the probable. This makes it possible to work on the future operationally without having to know it. For platforms this shift is decisive: a profile does not have to say what someone will do. It is enough to make different next connections differently probable. Probability turns ignorance into a comparable form. It reduces the future without fixing it – and precisely thereby prepares the selection.
