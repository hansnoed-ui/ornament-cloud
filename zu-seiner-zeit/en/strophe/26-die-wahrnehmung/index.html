Perception

Perception makes the boundary between body and consciousness questionable.

Perception makes a theoretical boundary palpable. For Luhmann it is a mental operation: consciousness sees and hears even when it does not reflect on it. The body enables and irritates this performance but cannot simply be equated with consciousness. From Merleau-Ponty's phenomenology another question can be asked: can the lived body be set over against perception as environment at all, if it already performs a pre-reflective relation to the world? This juxtaposition is constructed by the stanza; it is not a direct exchange between the two authors. Precisely their difference shows how much depends on where the boundary between body and consciousness is drawn.
