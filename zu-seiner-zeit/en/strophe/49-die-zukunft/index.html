The Future

The future is the preserved room to go on otherwise than is already expected.

Later events will occur, however open an order is today. In this project, future means more: the present room for continuations that are not yet fully expected or prepared. Every order must restrict possibilities in order to be able to act. It endangers its own capacity for a future when it can treat deviations only as errors and can no longer reach alternatives. Openness consists neither in the arbitrary nor in the assurance of a better outcome. It shows itself in whether experiences, objections and new conditions can change a continuation. The future of the stanza is this preserved surplus of going on otherwise over expectation that is already organised.
