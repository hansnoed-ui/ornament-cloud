The Connection

Continuity is not a state – it is the most improbable of all operations.

The real achievement of a system lies not in producing something but in carrying on. Every operation vanishes in the moment it is performed. Duration therefore arises not from persistence but from resumption. What we call identity is nothing other than the successful habit of connecting the next step to the previous one. Every failed connection, by contrast, opens up a different history. Systems therefore live not on stability but on the improbable ability to turn their own breaking off into continuation, again and again.
