Art

Art keeps perception socially reusable by keeping its difference open.

Art is neither material, form nor work, neither style, audience nor exhibition. It arises when these differences act back on one another in such a way that perception can be taken up socially without being exhausted in an unambiguous message. Material resists, form distinguishes, the work binds to an address, style produces expectation, the audience cultivates sensitivity, the exhibition orders relations. None of these operations possesses art on its own. Only their recursive coupling keeps open a domain in which something can be repeated without remaining the same. Art therefore preserves no meaning. It organises the possibility that perception returns differently.
