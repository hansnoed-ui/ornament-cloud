The Platform

The platform returns calculated possibilities as environment and processes their consequences.

The platform links profiles, data, calculations, recommendations and feeds into a feedback loop. It observes behaviour, condenses it into patterns and provides selected possibilities as a new environment. This environment influences further actions, which can be observed again. The circle remains open: people, institutions, events and technical errors produce deviations. Where systems additionally generate texts, images or answers, these outputs too become possible next occasions for communication. The platform thus organises not only visibility but changes what can be connected to. Its predictions gain effect by helping to shape the present against which they will later be tested.
