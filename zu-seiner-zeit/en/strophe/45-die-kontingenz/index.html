Contingency

Contingency becomes a chance for the future where alternatives remain within reach and structures remain revisable.

Contingent is what is so and could also be otherwise. This possibility of being otherwise is not yet a practically available alternative. An order can be changeable while people have no access to the means of changing it. Expectations, rules and probabilities limit the next connections; in doing so they make decisions possible. What matters is whether such limitations remain open to being worked on. An undetermined situation offers as little hold as one that excludes every deviation. The future needs structures that allow selection and make revision possible. The question of contingency therefore leads on to the question of who can actually recognise, try out and reach alternatives.

    Niklas Luhmann

    works on the concept of contingency

    Contingent is what is neither necessary nor impossible. Structures reduce complexity and at the same time carry along a horizon of other possibilities.

  2. Philosophy

    Judith Butler

    works on the repetition of norms

    Norms stabilise themselves through repetition without thereby becoming necessary. It is precisely repetition that allows variation and displacement.

  3. Art

    Mona Hatoum

    works with displaced everyday objects and spaces

    Familiar forms remain recognisable and at the same time appear as possible otherwise; what is taken for granted becomes unstable.

  4. Literature

    Milan Kundera

    Die unerträgliche Leichtigkeit des Seins

    Because a life cannot be repeated on trial, necessity and chance remain undecidably entangled.
