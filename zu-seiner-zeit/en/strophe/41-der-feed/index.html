The Feed

The feed makes prediction the present environment of its next verification.

The feed orders recommendations into a present. It does not simply show what exists but produces a continuous environment from possibilities that have already been weighted. Every selection appears as a now: visible, clickable, open to comment, skippable. Yet this now is calculated from the past and designed for further production of data. The feed does not only predict behaviour; it creates the situation in which the predicted behaviour can take place. Prediction thereby becomes recursive. What is shown influences what is done; what is done changes what can be shown in future. The feed is the present of a prediction that supplies its own preconditions after the fact.
