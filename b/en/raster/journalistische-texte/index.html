Test Grid for Journalistic Texts

Event, context, transition, cross-check, system references.

Read promptThe grid (text)Page with instructions

The prompt and the papers are in German. Add «Answer in English» to your material; the grid works the same.

How does a journalistic text turn selected material into a statement, which distinctions carry this statement, and what could limit or change its validity? Twelve procedures of cross-checking and a fillable case sheet. In progress, version 0.4.

How the grid works

UnityOperationMediation

How to apply it

  1. Copy the prompt. The whole application prompt 0.4.0 is then on your clipboard; if you prefer reading, open it as text.
  2. Paste it into an AI assistant and add your material: An article, a report, a commentary. The prompt reads it along event, context and transition and does the cross-check.
  3. Read what the grid finds: event, context and transition of the text, the twelve procedures of cross-checking and the completed case sheet.
