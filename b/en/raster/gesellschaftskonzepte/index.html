Test Grid for Concepts of Society

Unity, operation, mediation.

Read promptBackground paper (PDF)Page with instructions

The prompt and the papers are in German. Add «Answer in English» to your material; the grid works the same.

A low-threshold pre-filter for approaches that claim a diagnosis of society: what counts as the unity of society, which operation keeps it running, and how is mediation between the two achieved? In progress, version 0.4.

How the grid works

UnityOperationMediation

How to apply it

  1. Copy the prompt. The whole application prompt 0.4.0 is then on your clipboard; if you prefer reading, open it as text.
  2. Paste it into an AI assistant and add your material: A book, an essay, a programme that says how society is or should be. The prompt checks whether the three questions are answered before it reads on.
  3. Read what the grid finds: whether unity, operation and mediation are named, where the approach stays silent and whether it is worth reading on as a diagnosis of society.
