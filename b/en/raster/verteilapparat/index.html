The Distribution Apparatus of the Body

Whose bodily event gets how far?

Read promptBackground paper (PDF)Page with instructions

The prompt and the papers are in German. Add «Answer in English» to your material; the grid works the same.

A test instrument for whose bodily event gets how far, under which description, who can correct it and what materially follows. Seven thresholds, voice against measurement, four fields: being at the mercy, negotiable participation, disappearance, privileged opacity.

How to apply it

  1. Copy the prompt. The whole application prompt 2.1.0 is then on your clipboard; if you prefer reading, open it as text.
  2. Paste it into an AI assistant and add your material: A case from medicine, care, school, sport, administration or art in which a body reports something. The prompt follows the report across the thresholds and shows where it disappears or prevails.
  3. Read what the grid finds: the path of the report across the seven thresholds, where it disappears or prevails, and the field in which it lands.
