Curatorial projects

Thinking, ordering and hanging exhibitions.

Start the briefingWrite directly

From concept to closing: selecting works, reading rooms, writing texts, negotiating with artists and hosts, building the hanging, thinking the education along. Most recently «Supervisionen», six months in the Oberer Turm in Kaiserstuhl with nine artists, for Pro Kaiserstuhl and the municipality of Zurzach (2026), before that a year of clients' works at the Behmen in Aarau, for the BKS Aargau and the FARO Foundation (2024).

For municipalities, art spaces, galleries, foundations, companies with rooms and artists who do not want to carry an exhibition alone.

How it works

  1. A walk through the space, with the theme in mind.
  2. Concept, selection and programme on paper, budget and dates included.
  3. Installation, opening, accompanying programme, dismantling.
